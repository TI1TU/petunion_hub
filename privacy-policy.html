Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PetUnion Hub (petunionhub.com). We are a dedicated pet information and community blog platform. We respect your personal privacy and are committed to protecting any information you choose to share with us while reading our guides or subscribing to our newsletters.

2. Information We Collect

Because PetUnion Hub is an informative blog and content publishing platform (not an e-commerce store), we do not collect payment card information, billing addresses, or commercial transaction data. We may collect:

  • Newsletter Subscriptions: Your email address when you voluntarily sign up for pet care guides.
  • Contact Inquiries: Your name, email, and message content when submitting questions or pet rescue stories.
  • Standard Analytics: Anonymous browser user-agent, device screen category, and viewed pages to improve reading experience.

3. How Your Information is Used

We use your information exclusively to deliver requested pet newsletters, respond to editorial inquiries, and refine our educational pet guides. We never sell, rent, or distribute your email address to third-party marketing brokers.

4. Cookies and Web Storage

We use lightweight, functional cookies and local browser storage to remember your reading preferences (such as category filter selections). You can disable cookies at any time through your browser settings without impacting your ability to read articles.

5. Contacting Our Data Privacy Team

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to unsubscribe from our newsletter, contact us at hello@petunionhub.com.
