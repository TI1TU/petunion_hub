THE SACRED ARCHITECTURE OF RESHAM

FIGURE 01 — SILHOUETTE AYODHYA ZARDOZI ATELIER MEHRAULI & VARANASI
“A garment is not merely tailored; it is consecrated through generations of karigari hands.”

Along the eternal ghats of Varanasi, master weavers have practiced the Kadwa handloom tradition for over six centuries. We honor this sacred lineage by marrying monumental architectural structure with weightless silk tissue that breathes with regal composure.

Each bridal silhouette requires over eight hundred karigar hours of hand-beaten gold zardozi wire couching. The structural seams flow uninterrupted from high pagoda bandhgala collars to the sweeping floor-grazing hem.

SPECIMEN NO. 01 — 24K ZARDOZI WIRE & KATAN SILK

Hand-beaten metallic bullion wire couched on 420 GSM pure Varanasi katan silk over 820 artisan hours.

ORIGINE: VARANASI WEAVERS & MEHRAULI ATELIER
Chapitre II • Matière SRINAGAR 34.0837° N — 74.7973° E ÉDITION HÉRITAGE INDE

METALLIC RESHAM & MONOLITHIC TAILORING

“Weight is sovereignty. The tactile dignity of hand-spun raw silk honors the soul that inhabits it.”

Dense, raw, and uncompromised by synthetic acceleration. Sourced from indigenous silkworm cocoons in Bhagalpur and nomadic Changthangi shepherds in the high Himalayas, our fibers embody centuries of living craft.

Rather than fading with time, each couture piece matures like antique temple bronze, deepening its lustre through decades of intimate celebrations and royal heritage.

SPECIMEN NO. 02 — CHANGTHANGI CASHMERE & SOZNI

12-micron pure mountain goat fleece with single-strand needlework. Geographical Indication certified.

ORIGINE: LADAKH NOMAD GUILD & ATELIER SRINAGAR

01 / TISSUE VARANASI PIT-LOOMS

Generational Pit-Loom Katan Silk

Woven on generational wooden pit-looms in sacred Kashi. Each metre requires up to 14 days of rhythmic foot-treadle labor, yielding a drape with liquid metal density.

02 / WIREWORK ROYAL ZARDOZI

Hand-Couched 24K Gold Bullion

Zero machine embroidery. Our karigars in Old Delhi and Varanasi hand-couch real silver and gold-plated bullion wire using needlepoint dabka over floating canvas frames.

03 / HEIRLOOM KASHMIRI SOZNI

Single-Strand Needlework Sozni

Intricate paisley medallions executed with microscopic needle precision on hand-spun Changthangi fleece. A single shawl represents six months of master needlework.
